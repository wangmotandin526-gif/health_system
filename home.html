About HealthSys

HealthSys is an IT Health Management System that lets patients book appointments with doctors, keep track of their medical records and read trusted health articles, while clinic staff manage doctors, appointments and patient data, all from one dashboard.

What you can do with HealthSys

One account, three roles: patient, doctor and admin.

Book appointments

Patients can browse doctors by specialty and availability, and book, view or cancel their own appointments online.

Track medical records

Doctors record diagnoses and prescriptions after a visit, and patients can see their own history at any time.

Role-based access

Patients, doctors and admins each see only what's relevant to them, enforced by the server, not just hidden in the UI.

Doctor directory

Every doctor's specialty, contact details and available days are kept in the database and shown to patients when booking.

Admin tools

Admins can add doctors, create staff accounts, and manage every user's role from the Account Management page.

Account security

Passwords are hashed, sessions use short-lived tokens, and a self-service "Forgot password" flow is built in.

Health tips and preventive care

Simple, plain-language advice on staying well.

Everyday health tips
Stay hydrated

Sip water through the day; pale-yellow urine is a good sign.

Keep moving

Aim for about 150 minutes of moderate activity a week.

Sleep well

Most adults need 7-9 hours. Keep a regular sleep routine.

Eat a balanced plate

Half vegetables and fruit, a quarter protein, a quarter wholegrains.

Wash your hands

20 seconds with soap prevents many common infections.

Know your numbers

Check blood pressure, cholesterol and blood sugar regularly.

Preventive care checklist

Catching problems early makes them easier to treat. Discuss what suits you with your doctor.

  • Annual check-up with your doctor
    Review your health, medicines and any concerns.
  • Blood pressure and cholesterol
    Check regularly, more often if you have risk factors.
  • Vaccinations up to date
    Flu, tetanus and others your doctor recommends.
  • Screening tests for your age
    Ask about cervical, breast, bowel and prostate screening.
  • Dental check every 6-12 months
    Plus brushing twice a day and cleaning between teeth.
  • Eye test every 1-2 years
    Earlier if your vision changes.
  • Skin check
    See a doctor about any mole that changes or bleeds.
  • Mental wellbeing
    Talk to someone if you feel low or overwhelmed for weeks.

This content is for general education only and is not a substitute for advice from your doctor.
